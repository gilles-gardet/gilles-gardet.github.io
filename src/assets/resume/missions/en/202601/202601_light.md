The mission for Air France KLM focused on developing two dedicated registries within their ecosystem, one for **MCP** servers and one for **A2A** (_agent to agent_) agents.  

This bespoke implementation sought to enhance governance through sophisticated permission controls, while seamlessly integrating with AFKLM’s specific needs.  
Both registries have their lifecycle driven by **Flowable workflows**, where every request to consume a server or an agent must be approved by a human before access is granted.  

The project united a skilled team comprising developers, a Product Owner, and a Business Analyst, all driven by a strong reliance on **infrastructure as code** principles to ensure consistency and automation throughout the process.
