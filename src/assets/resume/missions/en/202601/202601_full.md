Both registries rest on a shared governance foundation, guaranteeing a consistent experience whatever the exposed protocol.
  - The same declaration and validation model applies to MCP servers and A2A agents alike.
  - Fine-grained permission management reaches down to each individual server and agent.

**Flowable workflows** cover the entire lifecycle, from the declaration of a resource to its decommissioning.
  - Human validation of a consumption request takes the form of an approval task handled by the workflow engine.
  - Every step is tracked, making it possible to audit who granted which access and when.

The team gathers four developers, one Product Owner and one Business Analyst.
  - Continuous alignment of functional and technical requirements allows permanent adaptation to business needs.

**Infrastructure as code** automates deployments and places configurations under governance.
  - Leveraging modern technologies guarantees a scalable and maintainable solution over time.
