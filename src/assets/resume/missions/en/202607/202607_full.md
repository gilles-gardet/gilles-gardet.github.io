The **MCP server** implemented as part of the mission is what gives the agent a grip on the information system.
  - The exposed _tools_ and _resources_ give control over every registry: **SOAP** and **REST** services, **MCP** servers and **A2A** agents.
  - Access to the MCP server is secured with **OAuth** and exposed through **APIM**.

The **RAG** pipeline indexes a GitHub repository hosting the **OAS** files of the information system.
  - **Semantic chunking** splits specifications into coherent fragments rather than fixed-size ones.
  - **Multi query retrieval** and **hybrid search** significantly improve recall on freely worded questions.
  - Vectors are stored in **pgvector**.

Several mechanisms make the agent usable in production over the long run.
  - A **session memory** keeps the conversation thread across exchanges.
  - Dedicated **guardrails** prevent the agent from being diverted away from its assistance scope over registry resources.
  - **Message compaction** reduces the context sent to the model on long conversations.

The whole solution runs on an **AKS** cluster and consumes the models served by **Azure AI Foundry**.
  - Several models are used depending on the purpose: **GPT-5.4** and **Terra** for chat, **Luna** for guardrails and **text-embedding-3** for embeddings.
