This mission covers the delivery of an **AI agent** for the Air France KLM _Integration Portal_, designed to support users in their day to day usage of the registry resources.  

Built with **Spring AI**, the agent relies on its own **MCP server** (also implemented as part of the mission) to drive the **SOAP**, **REST**, **MCP** and **A2A** service registries.  
A **RAG** pipeline further lets it query the **OAS** specifications of the information system in natural language.  

Designed for daily use, it keeps the conversation thread, stays confined to its assistance scope and keeps its context under control over long exchanges.
